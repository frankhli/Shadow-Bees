About this place

Experience authentic Beijing life in our carefully restored hutong courtyard house. Located in the heart of Dongcheng district, you'll be steps away from the Forbidden City yet tucked away in a quiet traditional neighborhood.

The space has been modernized while preserving traditional architecture. You'll enjoy a private room with modern bathroom facilities - something not all hutong accommodations can offer.
